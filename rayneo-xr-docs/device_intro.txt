Device Introduction​

RayNeo X Series AR glasses provide developers with a hardware platform featuring full-color
display, high brightness, and extreme lightness. Combined with high-performance computing
and open interfaces, they empower efficient development and deployment of innovative AR
applications. ​

Glasses Overview​
RayNeo X3 Pro​

RayNeo X3 Pro is the flagship AR glasses released by RayNeo in 2025. Based on the new
Qualcomm Snapdragon AR1 platform, it adopts the world's smallest mass-produced full-color
MicroLED optical engine, significantly enhancing display brightness and color performance.
With lighter weight, upgraded interaction methods, and greatly enhanced AI capabilities and
application ecology, it targets high-end consumer and professional markets. ​

RayNeo X2​

RayNeo X2 is a consumer-grade true AR glasses innovatively launched by RayNeo, featuring a
lightweight integrated design. Equipped with the Qualcomm Snapdragon XR2 platform and a
MicroLED +光波导 (diffractive waveguide) display solution, it supports multiple interaction
methods (temple touch, smart ring, voice), making it suitable for daily wear and in-depth
developer experience. ​
